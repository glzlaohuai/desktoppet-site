# DesktopPet website

Register: brand. This is the public marketing, support and privacy website for DesktopPet, a native macOS 14+ companion app. The source application is maintained separately. The source application’s PRODUCT.md, DESIGN.md, README.md and reviewed six-language App Store materials were read before creating this site.

Visitors want to understand what the small desktop pet does, meet the seven original characters, understand optional purchases, and find reliable support and privacy information. The voice is playful, affectionate and unhurried. Care never becomes an obligation. Avoid nagging, fake scarcity, invented download links, pricing that is not supplied by Apple, or claims of an existing App Store release.

The orange cat is free. Shiba Inu, rabbit, hamster, Ragdoll, black cat and Corgi have permanent individual unlocks or a collection of exactly these six. Future additions are excluded; earlier purchases do not reduce the collection price. There is no subscription. Affection has five levels and never decays. Holidays follow system Region, independently of language. The app supports English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean, French and German. Names, preferences and affection remain local; Apple processes optional purchases.

This website must offer six fully rendered languages, mobile layouts, keyboard operation, reduced motion, visible support and privacy routes, and actual original artwork. Use the marketing repository’s GitHub Issues as the public contact channel. Never publish private review contact details or app signing material.
